Why it matters

Your laptop lives near an outlet. Its battery does not need to live at 100%.

A full charge is useful before a trip. At a desk, it is often unnecessary. Honor Battery Saver automatically keeps a gentler range and restores maximum runtime when you actually need it.

How it works

Set the rules once — profiles switch on their own

The app responds to AC power, Wi-Fi changes, startup, and resume. You do not need to remember charging limits or change them by hand.

  1. 01

    Add your networks

    Assign the right profiles to your home and office networks.

  2. 02

    Plug in

    On AC power, the app checks the first matching rule.

  3. 03

    Protect battery longevity

    A local service applies a gentler limit and confirms the result in diagnostics.

Privacy

No cloud. No telemetry.

Honor Battery Saver requires no cloud account, sends no telemetry, and stores no passwords, BSSIDs, or Wi-Fi scan history.

Before installing

Check compatibility

The app uses an undocumented HONOR OEM interface. Behavior can change after BIOS, driver, or PC Manager updates.

  • Windows 11 x64, build 22000 or newer
  • Compatible HONOR laptop with PC Manager
  • Microsoft .NET 10 Desktop Runtime x64

Run hardware diagnostics and read the disclaimer before enabling automatic use.
