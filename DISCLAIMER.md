## Experimental hardware control

The application uses an undocumented HONOR OEM WMI interface to request battery
charging profiles. That interface, the confirmed commands, and the related
registry values may differ between laptop models or change after BIOS,
firmware, driver, Windows, or HONOR PC Manager updates. Compatibility and the
accuracy or persistence of charging thresholds are not guaranteed.

Only the three commands documented in this repository are used, but a command
that worked on one verified device may behave differently or fail on another
device. The application does not replace manufacturer diagnostics, support, or
safety guidance.
